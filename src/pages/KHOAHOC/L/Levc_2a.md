       <p>We agree with Einstein’s Relativity that
         the space and time are not absolute, and the space and time are distorted
         by the strong gravitational field. It means that when the gravitational
         field changes, then space and time also change. But we can’t agree
         with the second postulate of Einstein’s Special Relativity, that the
         velocity of light is constant. In fact, the velocity of light depends
         on the gravitational field and it changes as space and time change. </p>
       <p>A proof for this is shown as follows: </p>
       <p>As you know, the gravitational field
           of a black Hole is very strong and it distorts space and time around
           it. If our measurements of space and time are km , (a kilometre) and
           s , (a second ), then a Black hole’s measurements of space and time
           will be km’= km.γ and s.γ . (Of which γ is the dilation coefficient.
           To understand it , please read “<b>Light velocity changes as space
           and time change</b>”, at website: <u><a href="https://pub-756b239a5ae84d4c8c8ff709f375d430.r2.devhttps://wbabin.net/feast/cuong27.pdf">https://wbabin.net/feast/cuong27.pdf</a></u> ) </p>
       <p>In a case of a Black Hole, the dilation
           coefficient: γ can reach ∞ . For example, a Black Hole’s time passage
           being s ( a second) is equivalent to our Earth’s time passage of a
           year. </p>
       <p>Now, we consider the velocity of light
           in the space of a Black Hole. It is easy to understand for us when
           we assume the experiments as follows: </p>
       <p>Assume that in the empty space of the
           universe, there are two points, which are A and B. A distance from
           A to B is length: AB=450,000,000 km. At a point: A is our Earth and
           at a point: B is a Planet with its mass equal to the mass of the Earth.
           It means that the gravitational fields of the Planet and the Earth
           are a same. So, the space and time’s measurements of the Planet and
           the Earth are also the same. At the Earth, we place a lamp to shine
           a ray of light to the planet and a clock in order to measure the time,
           t for the ray of light to move from A to B, after, that reflecting
           from B to return A. At the planet, we put a mirror so that the reflecting
           a ray of light returns to Earth. Please seeing the illustation at
           a <b>figure: 1 </b></p>

       <p>We turn on a lamp and calculate the time
           t when the ray of light moves from A (the Earth) to B ( a mirror at
           the Planet), after that it reflects to return A . Taking into consideration
           the principle of constancy of velocity of light, ( c=300,000km/s as
           second postulate of Einstein’s Special relativity ), we who are the
           observers on the Earth calculate a time t , which is :</p>
       <p>Time = 2 (Light
           path: AB /velocity: c ) </p>
       <p>t = 2(90,000,000km / (300,000 km/s)) = 600 s &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
       <p>In this case, time t when a ray of light
           moves from A to B and reflecting from B to return A with the velocity
           c=300,000km/s is t = 600 s . This is normal. But if we assume that
           at a point: B, the Planet is replaced by a Black Hole and the distance
           from A to B is still AB=90,000,000km, then the time for a reflected
           ray of light will be different, -not t = 600s. This is because space
           and time around a Black Hole are distorted by the gravitational field,
           so theybecome a space measurement: km’=km.γ and a time measurement:
           s’=s.γ . </p>
       <p>The first postulate of Einstein’s Special
           Relativity: “ The laws by which the states of a physical systems undergo
           change are not affected, whether these changes of state be referred
           to the one or the other of two systems of co-ordinates in uniform
           translatory motion.” We find that a Black Hole is equivalent to the
           system or the frame of reference when it moves with rapid velocity
           making achange in its space and time. And at a Black Hole, we must
           calculate every physical factor with a Black Hole’s measurement. If
           the observers on the earth calculate velocity with their measurement
           of space: km, and of time: s , then the observers on a Black Hole
           will also calculate velocity with their measurement of space: km’=
           km.γ and of time: s’=s.γ . That is why, if the velocity of light at
           the Earth is c=300,000km/s, then the velocity of light at a Black
          Hole will be c’=300,000km.γ/s.γ . </p>
       <p>With c’=300,000km.γ/s.γ in space of a
           Black Hole and is c=300,000km/s in space of the Earth, we always make
           mistake of calculating as follows:</p>
       <p> c’= 300,000
           km.γ/s.γ = c =300,000km/s </p>
       <p>I think that we can’t calculate c’ =
           c . Because with a length: AB=90,000,000 km and c’ = c , we are right
           to calculate the time passage: t when a ray of light moves from A
           (the Earth) to B (a Black Hole) and reflecting from B to A :</p>
       <p><b>t = 2.(AI/c + IB/c’)</b>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; </p>
       <p><b>t = 2.﴾45.000.000km/(300.000km/s)+45.000.000km/(300.000km/s)﴿ =
           600 s</b> </p>
       
       <p>A time passage of: t=600s in first case,
         (when a ray of light moves in the space of the Planet) and a result
         of time passing: t=600s in this case, (when a ray of light moves in
         space of a Black Hole) are a same. It means that there isn’t any change
         of time at a Black Hole. Because of this, Einstein’s Relativity is
         incorrect and we can’t say that time passage at a Black Hole is slower
         than a time passage at our Earth. </p>
       <p>In fact, the physical scientists always
           confirm that a time passing at a Black Hole is very slow and a formula
           of delated time t’=t.γ in Einstein’s Special Relativity is correct.
           Thus, we must accept an equation, which is:</p>
       <p> c’ = 300,000 km.γ/s.γ
           ≠ c = 300,000 km/s </p>
       <p>The observers on the Earth view and measure
           their velocity of light: c=300,000 km/s , is true. And the observers
           on a Black Hole also view and measure their velocity of light: c’=300,000
           km.γ/s.γ , is also a true. But c’ ≠ c , so the velocity of light:
           c’ in space of Black Hole is only similar to c , but c’ is not equal
           to c . That is why, we are right to declare that the velocity of light
           is not constant. </p>

       <p>We find that a ray of light can’t wind
           from I to B in the space of a Black Hole as such. Because the velocity
           of light is a vector, a ray of light can only move in straight lines.
           Further, the strong gravitational field of a Black Hole forces a ray
           of light to move straight to a Back Hole’s center. </p>
       <p>Because of the first postulate of Einstein’s
           Relativity, at a Black Hole we must calculate every physical factor
           with a Black Hole’s measurement. And a length IB=45,000,000km.γ on
           a black Hole is equivalent to a length IB=45,000,000km on the Earth.
           So, the observers on the Earth are right when calculating a time passage:
           t when a ray of light moves from A to B and reflecting from B to return
           A in the straight dirrection as follows:</p>
       <p><b>t =2.(AI/c + IB/c’)</b></p>
       <p> <b>t = 2.﴾45.000.000 km/(300.000 km/s) + 45.000.000 km.γ /(300.000
           km.γ/s.γ)﴿= 2.( 150 s + 150 s.γ)</b> </p>
       
       <p>How fast does a light in space of a Black
           Hole move as viewed by the observers on Earth? </p>
       <p>From the result of time: t’=t.γ = 150s.γ
           when a light moves from I to B in the space of a Black Hole as viewed
           by the observers on the Earth and in fact, the observers on the Earth
           only view a length: IB=45,000,000km, the velocity of light when it
           moves in space of a Black Hole can be calculated by :</p>
       <p><b> c’ = IB/t’ = 45,000,000km
           /150s.γ = 300,000 km/s.γ </b></p>

       <p>A ray of light when it moves in the space
           of a Black Hole is very slow, then how fast does a material body move
           in the space of a Black Hole when viewed by observers on Earth ? </p>
       <p>Assume that there is a material body,
           which moves in the space of the Earth with velocity: v= km/s. After
           that, a material body falls in space of a Black Hole. As a case of
           the velocity of light in space of a Black Hole, with the viewed by
           the observers in a Black Hole, that material body moves with velocity
           v’= km.γ/s.γ . But Regarding the viewing of the observers on the Earth,
           the velocity of a material body in space of a Black hole is v=km/s.γ
           . Because a length: km.γ in space of a Black Hole is contracted and
           it is only equivalent to a length: km as the viewing of the observes
           on the Earth. So, with the viewing of the observers on the Earth,
           the velocity of a material body in the space of a Black Hole is slower
           than velocity of a material body in space of the Earth. </p>
       <p>We should understand that the gravitational
           field of a Black Hole is very strong. So, if any material body falls
           in the space of a Black Hole, it will be converted to become the energy
           of a super nucleus as a formula: E=m.c2 infers. And as a material
           body falls near they Black Hole’s center, it is converted. Thus, there
          isn’t any material body in the Black Hole’s center. </p>
   
       <p><b>Conclusion </b> </p>
       <p>If we accept that time passage at a Black
           Hole is very slow, we will have to accept the velocity of light when
           it moves in space of a Black Hole is also very slow and almost motionless,
           even as it approaches the Black hole’s center. To accept this, we
           will may be able to understand further the nature of space and time
           as well asthe nature of the universe. </p>
       <p>Einstein’s relativity is correct when
           it illustrates the change of space and time. But Einstein’s Relativity
           is a failure when it confirms that the velocity of light is constant
           in the universe. In fact, if space and time are constant, then the
           velocity of light will be constant. </p>
