<p class="Tuaduoi">Pope seeks to 'liberate' Virgin Mary from the Mafia</p>

<p align="center"><img src="https://pub-756b239a5ae84d4c8c8ff709f375d430.r2.dev/TONGIAO/IMG/ITALY/Mafia_Maria.JPG" width="500" height="417" alt=""></p>
<p align="center" class="baochi">Giáo Hoàng Phanxicô làm phép lành cho một nhóm trí thức mới của Vatican đang tìm cách ngăn chặn Mafia và các nhóm tội phạm có tổ chức khai thác hình ảnh của Đức Trinh nữ Maria cho mục đích bất chính.</p>
       <p>ROME (AP) — Pope Francis is giving his blessing to a new Vatican think tank that is seeking to&nbsp;<b><i>prevent the Mafia and organized crime groups from exploiting the image of the Virgin Mary&nbsp;</i></b>for their own illicit ends.</p>
      <p>The Vatican’s Pontifical Marian Academy launched the think tank Friday at a conference titled&nbsp;<b><i>“Liberating Mary from the Mafia.”</i></b>&nbsp;It was a reference to the<b><i>&nbsp;historic relationship between the Italian mob and the Catholic Church,</i></b>&nbsp;and the popular<b><i>&nbsp;displays of Marian devotion by mobsters</i></b>&nbsp;in Italy and beyond.</p>
      <p>In a message from the pope read out at the start of the conference, held at Rome’s Museum of Civilizations, Francis said the religious and cultural image and patrimony of the Madonna “must be preserved in its original purity.”</p>
      <p>He said any popular displays of devotion to Mary must “conform to the message of the Gospel and the teachings of the church,” and that people participating in them must be true Christians who look out for others, and especially the poor. </p>
         <p><b><i>The Catholic Church in Italy has long been associated with the Mafia,</i></b> thanks in part to<b><i>&nbsp;their post-war common cause against communism</i></b>. While some Catholic priests have courageously opposed the mob — and paid for it with their lives —<b><i>&nbsp;others have been called to explain their celebration of funerals, weddings and other sacraments for mafia dons, acceptance of their donations and participation in their religious processions.</i></b></p>
        <p>St. John Paul II in 1993 famously demanded Mafiosi convert, change their ways or face the wrath of God’s final judgment as he made a historic visit to Sicily after the mob slayings of two leading anti-Mafia prosecutors.</p>
        <p>Francis has followed in that tradition, declaring that mobsters were “excommunicated” and saying mobsters can't live Christian lives because their lifestyle “blasphemes” God. Marian devotion is particularly important to Francis, history's first pope from Latin America, where displays of such popular piety are common.</p>
        <p>Francis' message to the new think tank, which includes clergy as well as law enforcement experts involved in the fight against organized crime, was dated Aug. 15, a major Catholic feast day devoted to Mary.</p>
